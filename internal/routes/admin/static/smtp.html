How to Get a Gmail App Password

Google does not allow regular account passwords for third-party SMTP. Follow these 4 quick steps:

  1. Go to your Google Account Security.
  2. Ensure 2-Step Verification is turned ON.
  3. Search for App Passwords in the search bar.
  4. Type app name Salen Mobile Guru and click Create.
  5. Copy the 16-letter password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop) and paste it into the SMTP Account Password above!
Tip: Use port 587 and security STARTTLS with host smtp.gmail.com.
